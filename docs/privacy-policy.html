Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 20, 2026

Overview

SwathTrak is a field mapping app that tracks spray coverage area using your device's GPS. Your privacy is simple: we don't collect any of your data.

Data Storage

All data — including GPS tracks, session history, and app settings — is stored locally on your device. Nothing is transmitted to our servers or any third party.

Location Data

SwathTrak requests access to your device's location to display your position on the map and record spray coverage paths. Location data never leaves your device. You can revoke location access at any time in your device's Settings.

Analytics & Tracking

SwathTrak does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising frameworks, or any third-party tracking services.

Accounts

SwathTrak does not require or support user accounts. There is no sign-up, login, or cloud sync.

Third-Party Services

SwathTrak uses OpenStreetMap tile servers to display map imagery. These requests are made directly by your device and are subject to OpenStreetMap's privacy policy. No personal data is shared with OpenStreetMap beyond standard HTTP requests for map tiles.

Children's Privacy

SwathTrak does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Reach out at support@swathtrak.com.
