App Name: SwathTrak

Subtitle (30 chars max):
GPS Spray Coverage Tracker

Description:
SwathTrak maps your spray coverage in real time. Start spraying, and your path is drawn on the map with accurate area calculations — so you know exactly what you've covered and what you haven't.

Built for ATV and UTV sprayers working pastures, fence lines, food plots, and fields of any shape.

KEY FEATURES

- Real-time GPS tracking with coverage area displayed on the map
- Swath width adjustment to match your spray boom
- Tank management — track coverage per tank and log refills
- Session history — save, name, and review past spray jobs
- Works in acres, hectares, square feet, or square meters
- Imperial and metric unit support
- No account required — all data stays on your device

HOW IT WORKS

1. Set your spray width
2. Tap Spray to start tracking
3. Drive your route — coverage appears on the map in real time
4. Tap Refill when you need a new tank
5. Tap Finish to save the session with area and tank totals

SwathTrak works offline once loaded. No subscription, no account, no data collection.
