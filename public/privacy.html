Open SwathTrak

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Location and coverage

SwathTrak uses your device’s precise location while the app is open to show your position on a satellite map. While spraying is active, it records GPS paths and timestamps to draw sprayer coverage and calculate area. Location updates also keep your position visible when spraying is paused. You can revoke location permission in your device’s settings.

The app keeps the screen awake while spraying. It does not provide background location recording; manually locking the device or switching apps may interrupt tracking. Device compass readings orient the map while spraying and are not saved.

Data stored on your device

GPS paths, coverage, session names and dates, tank records, entered spray volumes, and saved preferences are stored locally using app or browser storage (localStorage). The active session is saved for recovery. SwathTrak has no account system, cloud sync, or developer-operated server receiving these records.

Saved sessions remain until you delete them in the app or clear the app’s local data. You can cancel the current job to discard its coverage. Clearing app or browser storage removes locally stored records and preferences. Device backups may include app data according to your operating system and backup settings.

Map imagery and network requests

Satellite imagery is downloaded directly from Esri’s World Imagery service at server.arcgisonline.com. These requests disclose your IP address, standard network request information, and the map tile coordinates requested. Tile coordinates identify the area being viewed and can reveal your approximate location as the map follows you. SwathTrak does not upload saved GPS paths, session names, or tank records to Esri.

Esri processes these requests under its privacy policy. An internet connection is needed to load imagery that is not already cached. Opening external links or contacting support uses the service you choose; if you email support, we receive your email address and anything you send.

Analytics and advertising

SwathTrak does not include analytics, crash-reporting services, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not sell your coverage records or use them for advertising.
